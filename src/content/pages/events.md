## Introduction

A big part of the development of the Ecohydraulics community happens through
educational activities. These come in the form of university courses, workshops,
training activities, and other events. This page will grow to host information
about these different activities.

## In this section

- [Training](/training/) — training opportunities and educational programs.

## Past Educational Events

**Short Course: Fish habitat modeling — MesoHABSIM tool and CFD-based fish
habitat models (2019).** The Technical University of Munich, in collaboration
with the Rushing Rivers Institute (USA) and the S. Sakowicz Inland Fisheries
Institute (Poland), offered a short course on the MesoHABSIM aquatic habitat
modeling approach and accompanying software. Lecturers: Piotr Parasiewicz and
Peter Rutschmann.

**AMBER & FIThydro webinar: Smart Ways To Improve River Connectivity (2020).**
A 3-hour webinar with 12 speakers from around Europe discussing river
fragmentation and better ways to manage and mitigate barrier impacts, as the
AMBER project unveiled Europe's first map of river barriers.
