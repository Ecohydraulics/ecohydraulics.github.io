The editorial board of the Journal of Ecohydraulics consists of two independent
groups, each with its own function.

## Advisory Editors

The advisory editors are responsible for high-level visioning and strategic
planning. This group addresses topics such as what content should be covered by
the journal and what activities the journal should lead.

Currently, we have a small advisory group, pending new additions in the months
to come. The current advisory editors are:

- Vlad Nikora, University of Aberdeen, UK
- Christos Katopodis, Katopodis Ecohydraulics Ltd, Canada
- Paul Kemp, University of Southampton, UK

## Associate Editors

The associate editors run the daily operations of the journal, overseeing peer
review of article submissions. They also contribute higher-level ideas that can
