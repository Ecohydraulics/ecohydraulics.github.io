The [Early Careers on Ecohydraulics Network](https://ecoenet.boku.ac.at) (ECoENet) is a nonprofit international research & career network consisting of **early career researchers** working in ecohydraulics and related fields.

[Visit the ECoENet website](https://ecoenet.boku.ac.at) to learn more, read their blog, sign up for the ECoENetter newsletter, and find out how to join.
