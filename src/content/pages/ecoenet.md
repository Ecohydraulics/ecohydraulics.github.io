The [Early Careers on Ecohydraulics Network](https://ecoenet.boku.ac.at)
(ECoENet) is a nonprofit international research network consisting of early
career researchers working within the interdisciplinary field of ecohydraulics.
It engages early career researchers in the development and consolidation of
ecohydraulics as an interdisciplinary field of research.

[Visit the ECoENet website](https://ecoenet.boku.ac.at) to learn more, read
their blog, sign up for the ECoENetter newsletter, and find out how to join.
