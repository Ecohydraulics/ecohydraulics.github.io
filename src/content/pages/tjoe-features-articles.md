- Muñoz-Mas et al., 2017. [Microhabitat competition between Iberian fish species and the endangered Júcar nase (Parachondrostoma arrigonis; Steindachner, 1866)](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/24705357.2016.1276417)
- **Enders et al., 2017.** [The effects of horizontally and vertically oriented baffles on flow structure and ascent performance of upstream-migrating fish](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/24705357.2017.1288555)

- Lancaster, 2018. [What is the right scale? Encouraging fruitful engagement for ecology with ecohydraulics](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/24705357.2018.1535260)

If you consider the topics of these articles, you will see that the journal is effectively spanning the major issues that our community works on. This includes basic science questions like (i) how do fish move and where do they choose to locate themselves, and (ii) what are the patterns and processes of water, sediment, vegetation, and heat flux. It also addresses societal problems such as fish passage, river restoration, and e-flows. The journal is working - the key is to expand submissions so that more of our community takes the opportunity to
publish in our native journal.
