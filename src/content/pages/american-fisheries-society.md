The **AFS-BES/ASCE-EWRI Joint Committee (JC) on Fisheries Engineering and
Science** was established to foster continued communication and promote joint
efforts in the area of fish passage, with support from the Bioengineering
Section of the American Fisheries Society (AFS-BES) and the American Society of
Civil Engineers' Environmental and Water Resources Institute (ASCE-EWRI).

Visit the
[Joint Committee on Fisheries Engineering and Science](https://fishpassagejointcommittee.fisheries.org)
to learn more about their mission, committees and task groups, webinars,
conferences, awards, and the Joint EWRI–AFS Fish Passage Reference Database.
