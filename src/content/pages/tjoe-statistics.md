The topic of metrics is something people want to know about, but we always have to remember that the articles make the journal more than the journal makes the articles. Simply putting your submission into a high-metric journal will not necessarily bring more readers and citations, because that does not make much difference for "discoverability". In other words, people have to find and read your article before they can cite it. That means that article SEO strategies are more important than journal metrics.

## Word Cloud

One useful way to think about TJoE is to consider the word cloud that comes from an analysis of article titles. That can help you determine whether the journal covers the topic you are working on — or maybe you have a topic that fits ecohydraulics but has been underserved so far.
