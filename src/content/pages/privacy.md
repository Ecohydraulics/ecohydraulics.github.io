description: "How this website handles your data, storage, and cookies."
---

This website is a static, community-run site. We do **not** run advertising, and we do **not** use analytics or tracking cookies. We try to keep data collection to the minimum needed to make the site work.

## Strictly necessary storage

To function, the site stores a few small preferences in your browser's local storage on your device. These are not transmitted to any server and are not used to track you:

- your **theme / display settings** (e.g. light or dark mode);
- your chosen **wallpaper / banner** preference;
- whether you have **dismissed the announcement** banner;
- your **cookie-consent choice** (so we don't ask again on every page).

Because these are strictly necessary or set at your own request, they do not require consent.

## Optional third-party features (consent required)

Two optional features contact third-party services, so they load **only if you choose "Accept all"** in the cookie banner. You can decline them with "Reject optional" and the site will work normally.

- **On-page translation.** If enabled, the translation widget loads a script from a content delivery network and sends the text to be translated, along
  with your IP address, to external machine-translation services (Microsoft and the translate.js service). It also stores your selected   language in a cookie. If you decline, no translation script or cookie is loaded - you can still use your browser's built-in translation.
- **Web fonts.** If enabled, a custom font is loaded from Google Fonts, which transmits your IP address to Google. If you decline, the site uses the fonts already installed on your device.

## Links to other sites

Pages on this site link to external resources (for example the IAHR website, the *Journal of Ecohydraulics*, YouTube, and LinkedIn). Those sites have their own privacy policies, which govern any data they collect once you visit them.

## Changing your choice

You can change your decision at any time using the **"Cookie settings"** link in the footer, which re-opens the consent banner.

## Contact

For questions about this website and how it handles data, please contact the Ecohydraulics community.
