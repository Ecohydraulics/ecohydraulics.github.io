## Fish Passage 2027 -- Call for Abstracts and Award Nominations

The **International Fish Passage Conference 2027** will take place **June 7 to 11, 2027 in Leeuwarden, the Netherlands**. This flagship even from the [Joint Committee on Fisheries Engineering and Science](https://fishpassagejointcommittee.fisheries.org/) brings researchers, practitioners, and decision-makers together to exchange ideas and advance fish passage, river connectivity, and ecological restoration. The conference returns to Europe for the first time since 2015.

## Share your research and experience

Abstract submissions are open across a broad range of topics relevant to the ecohydraulics community, including:

- Fish migration, behavior, and population restoration
- Fish passage solutions, monitoring, and new technologies
- Barrier removal and river connectivity
- Estuarine and transitional-water connectivity
- Climate-change impacts, policy, cooperation, and social dimensions of restoration

**Abstract submission deadline: October 31, 2026.**

[Explore the conference topics and submit an abstract](https://www.fishpassage2027.com/abstract-submission/).

## Nominate outstanding people and projects

Nominations are also open for two Fish Passage 2027 awards:

- **Career Achievement Award** — recognizing a professional's significant and lasting contribution to fish passage.
- **Distinguished Project Award** — recognizing an innovative, technically excellent project with demonstrated ecological benefits for fish passage, river connectivity, and habitat.

[Learn about the awards and submit a nomination](https://www.fishpassage2027.com/awards/).

## A special Dutch opportunity

The conference is planned to coincide with the opening of the **Fish Migration River** at the Afsluitdijk, a project designed to reconnect the Wadden Sea and Lake IJsselmeer for migratory fish. Participants will have an opportunity to visit the project area as part of the conference program. So:

**Save the date: June 7--11, 2027, in Leeuwarden, the Netherlands,** and check out the [official website of the Fish Passage Conference 2027](https://www.fishpassage2027.com/).
