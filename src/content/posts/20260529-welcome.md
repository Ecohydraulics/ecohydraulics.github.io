Welcome to the new home of the **Ecohydraulics community**! This website brings together the people, ideas, and resources that connect researchers and
practitioners working at the interface of ecology and hydraulics around the world.

## What you'll find here

- **[About](/about/)** — the history and organization of the community and the IAHR Ecohydraulics Technical Committee.
- **[Community](/community/)** — conferences, awards, jobs, allied organizations, including the [Early Careers on Ecohydraulics Network](/ecoenet/).
- **[Education](/events/)** — courses, workshops, training, and learning   resources.
- **[Research](/research/)** — the *[Journal of Ecohydraulics](/journal/)* and a   [software guide](/software-guide/) for the field.

## Get involved

This site is *from the community, for the community*. If you have content, events, job postings, resources to share, or a viewpoint on [what ecohydraulics is](/what-is-it/): we'd love to hear from you. Follow us on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ecohydraulics9581) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ecohydraulics) to stay up to date.
