## Early-bird deadline extended: more time to sort your Lausanne plans

Good news for everyone still wrestling with travel forms, budget approvals, train tickets, flight prices, hotel tabs, institutional portals, or the general admin swamp that comes with conference season: the early-bird registration deadline for the Ecohydraulics conference in Lausanne has been extended to **May 15**.

Registration is available here: <https://ise2026.epfl.ch/registration/>

So, if Lausanne is on your summer map, this is the moment to lock things in. Presenters, poster people, session-hoppers, river nerds, model wranglers, field-data survivors: get registered, sort the travel, and we will see you by the lake.
