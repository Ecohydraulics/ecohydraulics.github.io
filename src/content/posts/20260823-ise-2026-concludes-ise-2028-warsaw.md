## A strong week in Lausanne

The 16th International Symposium on Ecohydraulics (ISE) closed on **20 August 2026** at the SwissTech Convention Center on the EPFL campus. Approximately **300 delegates** attended this first ISE held in Switzerland. Our thanks go to the Local Organizing Committee under [Paolo Perona](https://people.epfl.ch/paolo.perona?lang=en) and [Giovanni De Cesare](https://people.epfl.ch/giovanni.decesare?lang=en) as well as their fantastic team for a well-run and scientifically dense programme.

## Next stop: Warsaw, 2028

The 17th International Symposium on Ecohydraulics will convene **25 June to 1 July 2028** on the campus of the Warsaw University of Life Sciences (SGGW). It is organized jointly by SGGW, the National Inland Fisheries Research Institute, and the Institute of Geophysics of the Polish Academy of Sciences.

The theme is **“Ecohydraulic Upscaling for Nature Restoration”**: moving ecohydraulic methods from site-scale application toward regional and continental strategy, against the backdrop of the EU Nature Restoration Law. The symposium is co-chaired by [Piotr Parasiewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piotr_Parasiewicz) ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1672-3759)), originator of the MesoHABSIM mesohabitat simulation system, together with Tomasz Okruszko (SGGW) and Paweł Rowiński (IG PAS).

Programme, sessions, and registration details are published at <https://ise2028.sggw.edu.pl/>, and enquiries go to <ise2028@sggw.edu.pl>.

Our [Conferences page](/conferences/) has been updated with both entries, and ISE 2026 now appears in the list of past symposia. Save the date and see you in Poland!
