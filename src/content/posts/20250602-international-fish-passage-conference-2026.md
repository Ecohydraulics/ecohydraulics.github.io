## International Fish Passage Conference 2026

![International Fish Passage Conference 2026](https://fishpassage2026.ucdavis.edu/sites/g/files/dgvnsk15976/files/styles/sf_hero_banner/public/media/images/fishpassage-hero-4.jpg)

**Hosted by the University of California, Davis, at the Campus Conference Center — May 4–8, 2026.**

The organizing committee is thrilled to share their progress. They are currently seeking sponsors; if you are interested in learning more, please visit the [Sponsorship Information and Registration](https://fishpassage2026.ucdavis.edu/sponsors) page.

For more general information, see the [Fish Passage 2026 website](https://fishpassage2026.ucdavis.edu/), or contact the organizing committee by email at [InfoFishPassage2026@ucdavis.edu](mailto:InfoFishPassage2026@ucdavis.edu).

### Conference deadlines

- **Sep 1, 2025** — Session and Workshop Proposal Deadline
- **Oct 15, 2025** — Notification of Session and Workshop Proposal Acceptance
- **Nov 17, 2025** — Registration Opens
- **Dec 1, 2025** — Talk and Poster Abstracts Deadline
- **Jan 15, 2026** — Distinguished Project Award Nomination Deadline
- **Feb 1, 2026** — Notification of Talk and Poster Acceptance
- **Feb 15, 2026** — Regular Registration Deadline

The organizing committee is looking forward to welcoming you to UC Davis!
